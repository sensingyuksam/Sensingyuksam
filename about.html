About Sensing Yuksam

Yuksam told by the people who live here.

How it started

In June 2026 the Khangchendzonga Conservation Committee (KCC) in Yuksam and the Futuring Heritage project at Leiden University invited people from Yuksam to an informal meeting at the KCC office. There was no finished plan. The question was simple: what does Yuksam mean to you?

Everyone was asked to bring something that could answer it: a photograph, an object, a story, a memory, a song, a drawing. Those things started the conversation about what should be remembered and how it should be shown.

At that meeting, people from the community and the Panchayats suggested that the young people of Yuksam should lead the project. A second meeting, for young people, followed on Sunday 16 August 2026, to decide together what Sensing Yuksam could become.

Who it belongs to

Sensing Yuksam belongs to the people of Yuksam. It is made with and by residents, not about Yuksam from the outside. Residents document their own place. Outsiders do not describe it for them.

The aim is to let people experience a place, not only to represent it: what Yuksam looks like, sounds like and feels like, through the eyes of the people who live here. It is not finished and never will be.

Films, photographs and recordings stay with the people who made them. Most are posted on the makers' own YouTube or Facebook, and this website links to them and shows the maker's name. Nothing is added without the maker's agreement, and anything can be taken down if the maker asks.

What it is made of

The website is organised in ten chapters, from People Who Make Yuksam to My Yuksam. Each chapter has ideas to film, photograph or record. The Yak spotlight is the first chapter to open. The project is also working towards a multimedia book that brings photographs, audio, video, stories and memories together.

Join the team

If you live in Yuksam and want to film, photograph, record or write, you are welcome on the team. You do not need special equipment. A phone is enough.

Pick an idea from any chapter, make it, and post it on your own YouTube or Facebook. Then send us the link. Everyone on the team is credited for what they make.
